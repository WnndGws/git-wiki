# Recipe

* No idea where she got it, but it's tasty

## Dry Mixture

* 4 cups whole meal self-raising flour
    * "I sometimes use 2 cups self-raising, 2 cups normal flour because I always
      run out of one of these"
* 2 cups (200 g) rolled Oats
* 4 cups crushed All Bran cereal.
    * "The one that looks like little worms" 😂
    * "I sometimes use whatever leftover cereal I have" (e.g. Wheetbix)
* 2-3 cups oats bran
* 4 teaspoons (20 ml) baking powder
* 1/2 teaspoon (2.5 ml) salt
* 1 cup sunflower seeds (OPTIONAL)
* 1 cup pumpkin seeds (OPTIONAL)
* 1 cup crushed roasted almonds (OPTIONAL)

## Wet mixture

* 1½ to 2 cups dark brown sugar
* 500 g butter or margarine
* 750 ml buttermilk
    * Use more if adding more dry ingredients (eg.
      more All Bran etc)
* 2 large eggs
* 1.5 "teelepels" (7ml) baking soda
