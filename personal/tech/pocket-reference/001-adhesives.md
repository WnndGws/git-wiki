## Reactive Adhesives
### Acrylic
* Two part curing (needs disposable surfaces to mix on)
* No real surface prep needed
* Hardens within 3-20min (Cures in 8 to 48hrs)
* Working temperature ~15-30degC
* Service range ~-40-120degC
* No need to clamp
* Tolerates moisture and unprepped surfaces well
* Good for filling spaces

### Cyanoacrylate (Super glue)
* Cures with water on piece or humidity in air
* Exothermic
* Very brittle
* Hardens in 5-30sec
* Working temperature ~5-40degC
* Service range ~-40-80degC
* Will capillary into material which can lower adhesion
* Strongest in thin layer, and clamped
* Baking soda can be used to turn into small gap filler

### Epoxy
* Two part curing (needs disposable surfaces to mix on)
* Best structural adhesive out of all glues
* Best temperature resistance out of all glues
* Hardens in 2-60min (Cures in ~24hrs)
* Working temperature ~15-35degC
* Service range ~-40-120degC
* Do NOT clamp (clamping will weaken the bond)
* Good gap filling

### Polyurethane
* Cures with water on piece or humidity in air
* Swells as it cures!
* Hardens in 20-30min (Cures in ~6hrs)
* Working temperature ~-10-45degC
* Service range ~-40-90degC
* Apply thin layer and clamp
* Good moisture resistance

### Silicone
* Cures with water on piece or humidity in air
* Low adhesive capability, used more as a sealant
* Hardens in 20-40mins (Cures in 24-72hrs, can quicken with higher temp)
* Working temperature ~5-40degC
* Service range ~-40-200degC
* No clamping
* Great for filling gaps

### Urethane
* Two part curing (needs disposable surfaces to mix on)
* Highly flexible
* Hardens in 2-120min (Full strength can take up to 7 days)
* Working temperature ~-10-45degC
* Service range ~-40-90degC
* No need to clamp
* Great impact resistance

## NON-Reactive Adhesives
### Aliphatic Resin (Yellow wood glue)
* Cures with evaporation of solvent (solvent is usually water)
* Hardens in 30-60min (Full strength in 24hrs)
* Working temperature ~10-30degC
* Service range ~-20-80degC
* Needs clamping
* Stronger than white wood glue

### Construction Adhesive
* Cures with evaporation of solvent
* Flexible when dry
* Hardens in 10-30mins (Full strength in 12-24hrs)
* Working temperature ~-5-50degC
* Service range ~-5-80degC
* No need to clamp
* Mixture of ingredients, making different specialities
* Excellent at filling gaps

### Contact Adhesive
* Cures with evaporation of solvent
* Hardens in 5-30mins (Full strength in ~24hrs)
* Working temperature ~5-40degC
* Service range ~-40-80degC
* No need to clamp
* Parts will harden together instantly!
  Be careful

### Hot glue
* Cures as it cools
* Works well on porous surfaces due to viscosity
* Hardens in 15-60sec (Full strength after 24hrs)
* Working temperature ~120-150degC
* Service range ~-20-80degC
* No clamping needed
* Good at filling gaps
* Bad in hot environments for obvious reasons

### PVA Glue (White wood glue)
* Cures by evaporation of solvent
* Good on porous materials
* Hardens in 5-10mins
* Working temperature ~-20-35degC
* Service range ~-20-80degC
* Clamp pieces
* Most other adhesives wont adhere to PVA after its cured
